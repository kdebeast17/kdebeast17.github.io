### Shidoku Solver using Gröbner Basis
*Course Project, May 2024*

Modeled a 4x4 Shidoku board as a system of polynomial equations; enumerated all valid completions of a partially filled board using Gröbner basis methods, proving the reduced Gröbner basis is triangular and the corresponding ideal is radical.
